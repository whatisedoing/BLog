        If your browser can't download them
        
Some browsers aren't allowed to download straight from Untappd's servers. In that case, download the photos on a computer with the small script Claude gave you. It saves each photo as a file named with its check-in number. Then choose all of those files here.

        
        

      

    


    

      
When every photo has been copied, erase the Untappd links that BeerLog has been holding. After that, nothing in your BeerLog points to Untappd.
